About us

A family shop, run the old way

Shri Ram has been making sweets and snacks for the neighbourhood for years. Pure ghee, fresh milk and no shortcuts. The kadhai is lit before sunrise so the first batch of kachori is ready when you are.

Come for a quarter kilo of jalebi on a Sunday morning, or order a full festival spread for the house. Either way, it is packed the same careful way.
